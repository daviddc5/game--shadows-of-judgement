# Character portrait prompts

**These portraits are AI-made and should be replaced.** They were most likely
generated with Google's Gemini: `detective-neutral.png`,
`detective-alternate.png` and `killer-neutral.png` carry Gemini's small
four-pointed sparkle watermark in the bottom-right corner (the tool itself
wasn't recorded). The game's music is also Gemini-made
(`public/assets/audio/README.md`).

The plan (see "Art style" in [DIRECTION.md](../DIRECTION.md)) is no AI art in
the shipped game: Deniz and Giovanni get redrawn by hand in the gothic manga
style, ideally with 3–4 expressions each, before any paid release or
crowdfunding. Until then, crop the watermark out if these portraits are used
anywhere public. The prompts below still work as a character brief for the
redraw (hair, eyes, scar, clothes, facing direction, background).

Files:

- `public/assets/images/DetectivePortraits/detective-neutral.png`: Deniz, in
  the game (prompt below).
- `public/assets/images/DetectivePortraits/detective-alternate.png`: the same
  Deniz in front of a night skyline; not used.
- `public/assets/images/KillerPortraits/killer-neutral.png`: Giovanni, in the
  game (prompt below).
- `art/drafts/mara-neutral.jpg`, `art/drafts/wren-neutral.jpg`: drafts for two
  more characters; not used, no watermark, prompts not recorded.
